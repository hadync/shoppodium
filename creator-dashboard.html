CONTENT TO POST

Use the format. Keep your own voice.

FORMAT 01 · CUSTOMER HANDOFF
“If you own a tint shop, stop handing customers their keys like this.”
Show the normal handoff first. Cut to the Brandr bag. Show the bag leaving in the customer’s car. End with your creator link.
FORMAT 02 · ONE CHANGE
“We changed one thing about our customer handoff.”
Start with the finished vehicle, then reveal the bag and what is inside. Keep the explanation short and visual.
FORMAT 03 · POV
“POV: you finally started treating your tint shop like a real brand.”
Fast cuts: finished install, bag, air freshener, cards, customer taking it home. No long explanation.
FORMAT 04 · CUSTOMER REACTION
“Our customers weren’t expecting this.”
Film the handoff reaction and let the product explain itself. Put your Brandr link in the caption or bio.
FORMAT 05 · THREE THINGS
“3 things every tint shop should do after finishing a customer’s car.”
Make Brandr one of three useful ideas instead of making the whole video an ad. This keeps the content native.
